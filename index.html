Artisan Embroidery Atelier

Where Every Thread Becomes Art.

Step inside our Coimbatore handloom embroidery room. Discover pure metallic zari, cutdana stones, resham threads, and master artisan craftsmanship.

The Discipline

The Geometry of Tamil Aari Needles

Unlike machine-stamped embroidery, authentic Aari work requires exceptional hand-eye coordination. Using a slender hooked needle, the artisan works from beneath the fabric while guiding fine metallic zari and bead strings from above.

The Atelier Journey

The Art of Bespoke Tailoring

Discover our 5-step precision crafting process that guarantees an effortless, sculpted fit.

01. Consultation & Styling Advisory

We discuss your occasion, drape your saree fabric, and advise on flattering necklines and back designs.

02. 14-Point Precision Measurements

Comprehensive body mapping covering shoulder pitch, armhole depth, and waist contours.

03. Handcrafted Aari Stenciling

Transferring your chosen motifs onto fabric stretched on wooden frames and embroidering gold zari.

04. Master Stitching & Reinforcement

Assembling with pre-washed pure cotton linings, flexible stays, and durable dori attachments.

05. Final Fitting & Handover

On-the-spot trial in our boutique ensuring total confidence for your celebration.
